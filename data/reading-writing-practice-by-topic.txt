RWI06
Researchers reported that students who used a new reading app scored higher on a comprehension test than students who did not. However, participation was voluntary, and students who selected the app had higher reading scores before the study began. Which follow-up design would most directly test whether using the app itself improves comprehension?
4
Ask students who already use the app whether they believe it helps them read more quickly.
Compare this year's voluntary app users with the highest-scoring students from the previous year.
Randomly assign students with similar starting scores to app and non-app groups, then compare their changes in comprehension scores.
