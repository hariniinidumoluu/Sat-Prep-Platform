Pythagorean Theorem with Algebra
Hard
A rectangular garden has a diagonal of 25 feet. Its length is 5 feet greater than its width. What is its area, in square feet?
250
275
300
350
C
Let w be the width, so the length is w + 5. By the Pythagorean theorem, w^2 + (w + 5)^2 = 625. Simplifying gives w^2 + 5w - 300 = 0, or (w + 20)(w - 15) = 0. The positive width is 15 and the length is 20, so the area is 300 square feet.
