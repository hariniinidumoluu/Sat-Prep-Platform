For a linear function f, f(-2) = 13 and f(4) = -5. What is the value of f(7)?
-5
-14
-8
7
B
The slope is (-5 - 13)/(4 - (-2)) = -18/6 = -3. Using f(x) = -3x + b and f(-2) = 13 gives 13 = 6 + b, so b = 7. Therefore, f(7) = -3(7) + 7 = -14.

The function A(t) = 600(0.75)^t models the amount of a substance remaining t hours after an experiment begins. What is the percent decrease in the amount of the substance from t = 1 to t = 3?
25%
56.25%
43.75%
75%
C
The time increases by 2 hours, so the amount is multiplied by (0.75)^2 = 0.5625. This means 56.25% of the original amount at t = 1 remains. The percent decrease is 100% - 56.25% = 43.75%.

Easy
A rectangle has a length of 12 centimeters and a width of 5 centimeters. What is the length, in centimeters, of its diagonal?
12
17
13
7
C
The diagonal forms the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 12 and 5. By the Pythagorean theorem, d^2 = 12^2 + 5^2 = 169. Therefore, d = 13 centimeters.

10π
36π

D

Two machines produce posters at constant rates. Machine A produces 4 posters per minute, and machine B produces 6 posters per minute. Machine A begins operating 12 minutes before machine B. How many minutes must machine B operate before both machines have produced the same total number of posters?
12
24
18
30
B
Let x represent the number of minutes machine B operates. Machine A operates for x + 12 minutes. Set the total numbers of posters equal: 4(x + 12) = 6x. Expanding gives 4x + 48 = 6x, so 48 = 2x and x = 24.

Let f(x) = ax + b, where a and b are constants. If f(-2) = 11 and f(f(-2)) = -15, what is the value of b?
7
-7
2
11
A
Since f(-2) = 11, the second condition means f(11) = -15. The line passes through (-2, 11) and (11, -15). Its slope is (-15 - 11)/(11 - (-2)) = -26/13 = -2. Using f(-2) = 11 gives 11 = -2(-2) + b, so b = 7.

Systems of Linear Equations in Context
Hard
A shop charges a fixed setup fee plus a constant price for each customized notebook. An order of 8 notebooks costs $130 after an $18 coupon is applied. An order of 12 notebooks costs $186 after an identical $18 coupon is applied. What would an order of 20 notebooks cost without a coupon?
$280
$298
$334
$316
D
Let p be the price per notebook and f be the fixed setup fee. Before the coupon, the first order costs 148 dollars and the second costs 204 dollars. Therefore, 8p + f = 148 and 12p + f = 204. Subtracting gives 4p = 56, so p = 14. Then f = 148 - 8(14) = 36. An order of 20 notebooks costs 20(14) + 36 = 316 dollars.

Quadratic Functions and Vertex Form
Hard
A quadratic function f satisfies f(1) = 12, f(7) = 12, and f(4) = -6. What is the value of f(0)?
18
26
32
10
B
Because f(1) = f(7), the axis of symmetry is halfway between 1 and 7, at x = 4. Write the function in vertex form as f(x) = a(x - 4)^2 - 6. Using f(1) = 12 gives 12 = 9a - 6, so a = 2. Thus f(0) = 2(0 - 4)^2 - 6 = 26.

Quadratic Discriminant and Unknown Constants
Hard
The quadratic equation x^2 - (k + 2)x + 4k = 0 has exactly one real solution, where k is a real constant. What is the sum of all possible values of k?
4
8
12
16
C
A quadratic has exactly one real solution when its discriminant equals zero. Therefore, (k + 2)^2 - 4(1)(4k) = 0. Expanding gives k^2 - 12k + 4 = 0. By the quadratic formula, the possible values are k = 6 + 4sqrt(2) and k = 6 - 4sqrt(2). Their sum is 12.

Problem-Solving & Data Analysis
Conditional Probability
Hard
A greenhouse grows 400 seedlings. Seventy percent belong to species A, and the remaining seedlings belong to species B. Ten percent of species A seedlings fail inspection, while 20% of species B seedlings fail inspection. If one seedling is selected at random from those that failed inspection, what is the probability that it belongs to species B?
3/10
1/2
3/5
6/13
D
There are 0.70(400) = 280 species A seedlings and 120 species B seedlings. Of these, 0.10(280) = 28 species A seedlings and 0.20(120) = 24 species B seedlings fail inspection. A total of 52 seedlings fail. Therefore, the probability that a randomly selected failed seedling belongs to species B is 24/52 = 6/13.

2
Geometry & Trigonometry
Chords and Coordinate Geometry
Hard
A circle in the xy-plane has center (6, -4) and radius 10. A vertical line x = k intersects the circle at two points that are 12 units apart. If k is greater than 6, what is the value of k?
8
14
12
16
B
The perpendicular from the circle's center to the vertical chord bisects the chord. Half the chord is 6 units long. Using the Pythagorean theorem with radius 10 gives the horizontal distance from the center to the chord as sqrt(10^2 - 6^2) = sqrt(64) = 8. Because the chord lies to the right of the center, k = 6 + 8 = 14.
